About vonchess

The Philippines' chess learning platform.

Our Mission

vonchess exists to make chess accessible to every Filipino. Whether you're picking up your first piece or preparing for tournament play, our platform provides the tools, community, and guidance you need to improve.

What We Offer

Interactive Lessons

Structured courses covering openings, middlegame strategy, and endgame techniques with interactive board exercises.

Tactics Training

Daily puzzles and tactical exercises to sharpen your pattern recognition and calculation skills.

Tournaments

Browse and register for chess tournaments happening across the Philippines, from local club events to major competitions.

Find a Coach

Connect with verified chess coaches for personalized training, whether you're a beginner or an advanced player.

Opening Explorer

Explore chess openings with an interactive database — study popular lines, variations, and key ideas.

Learning Roadmap

Follow a structured curriculum that takes you from fundamentals to advanced concepts at your own pace.

Built for the Philippine Chess Community

Chess has a rich history in the Philippines, from the legendary Eugene Torre to a thriving grassroots scene of clubs, schools, and online communities. vonchess is built to serve this community by providing a modern, locally-focused platform where players can learn, compete, and grow together.

We partner with local tournament organizers, coaches, and chess clubs to ensure our platform reflects the needs of Filipino players at every level.

Get Involved

Whether you want to organize a tournament, offer coaching, or simply improve your game, there's a place for you on vonchess.
