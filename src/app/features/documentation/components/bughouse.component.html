Bughouse Chess

Bughouse is a 2v2 real-time chess variant played across two boards simultaneously. Partners share captured pieces, creating a fast-paced, deeply strategic team experience. This guide covers the full lifecycle — from forming a team to playing a match.

Overview & Team Structure

A Bughouse match involves four players split into two teams of two (Team A vs Team B). Each team controls one board per player, and the boards are paired:

Team A

You — Board A, White
Your Partner — Board B, Black

Team B (Opponents)

Opponent 1 — Board A, Black
Opponent 2 — Board B, White

Partners share a piece pocket: pieces you capture on your board flow into your partner's reserve and vice versa. Coordination between partners is the core strategic layer of Bughouse.

Invite System

Before entering the match queue you must have an accepted partner. The lobby provides a real-time invite flow built on WebSockets — no page refreshes needed.

1. Sending an Invite

  1. Open the Bughouse Lobby and switch to the Game tab.
  2. Type any part of a username in the Invite Player search bar — results show only currently online users.
  3. Click Invite next to the player. An entry with a spinning indicator • Pending status will appear in the Sent Invitations outbox.
  4. You can send invites to multiple players simultaneously and cancel any pending invite at any time with the Cancel button.

2. Receiving an Invite

Incoming invitations appear as a floating toast stack in the top-right corner of any page — you do not need to be on the Bughouse page to see them. Each toast shows:

  • The sender's username and their current rating.
  • A 2v2 Partner badge confirming the invite type.
  • Accept and Reject action buttons.

Accepting an invite sets you as the inviter's partner and fills the Partner slot in their lobby. Any other pending invites they sent are automatically invalidated. Both players see the partner slot update in real time.

3. Invite Lifecycle & Edge Cases

Event Outcome
Receiver accepts Sender's outbox shows Accepted; partner slot fills instantly
Receiver rejects Outbox shows Rejected; sender can invite someone else
Sender cancels a pending invite Toast disappears from receiver's screen immediately
Partner slot already filled The Invite button is disabled for the current partner; sender must kick them first
Captain kicks partner Slot empties; both sides see the update in real time via WebSocket push

Matchmaking Queue

Once your partner slot is filled, the Find Bughouse Match button activates. The queue is team-based — both you and your partner enter as a unit and are matched against another complete team of two.

Entering the Queue

  1. Click Find Bughouse Match. The UI transitions to the Searching state — a radar pulse animation and a live elapsed-time counter (queueTime() in seconds) are shown.
  2. A real-time status message (queueStatus()) is broadcast from the server — it may update to reflect queue depth, estimated wait, or partner sync state.
  3. You can click Cancel Search at any time to leave the queue. Your partner slot is preserved — you do not need to re-invite.

Match Found & Countdown

When the server pairs two full teams, both sides transition to the Match Found screen which displays:

  • All four players (Team A on the left, Team B on the right) with their ratings.
  • A 5-second SVG ring countdown. When it reaches zero the game starts automatically.

No manual "ready" confirmation is required — the countdown handles synchronization for all four players.

Gameplay Mechanics

Piece Pockets & Drops

Above and below each board you will see a Pocket row displaying the available piece inventory for both players on that board. Each piece tile shows a count badge.

  • Receiving pieces: When your partner captures a piece on their board, it instantly appears in your pocket (count increments in real time).
  • Dropping pieces: On your turn, click any piece in your pocket with a count > 0. A green-dot overlay grid appears on the board — click a valid empty square to complete the drop. This uses your full turn.
  • Drop restrictions: Pawns cannot be dropped on the 1st or 8th rank. A promoted pawn reverts to a pawn when re-captured and passed to the opponent's partner.
  • Cancelling a drop: Click the same piece tile again (or any other square outside the grid) to deselect without dropping.

Clocks & Time Control

Each player has an independent clock displayed next to their name on the board panel. Clocks turn red when fewer than 20 seconds remain. Running out of time ends the entire match — not just the individual board.

Win Conditions

  • Checkmate on either board ends the match instantly in favour of the checkmating team.
  • Flag fall (clock expiry) on any player ends the match instantly in favour of the opposing team.
  • Results are team-wide — winning one board wins the match for both partners.
