How to play Bughouse

1. Teams and Boards

Bughouse is played on two boards side-by-side by two teams of two players (2v2). White on Board A is partners with Black on Board B. Black on Board A is partners with White on Board B.

2. Captured Pieces & Pockets

Whenever you capture a piece on your board, it is immediately passed to your partner's reserve pool ("pocket") on the other board. They can hold these pieces indefinitely and choose to drop them later.

3. Drop Mechanics

On your turn, instead of making a normal chess move, you can choose to "drop" any piece from your pocket onto any empty square on your board. This counts as your move for the turn.

  • Pawns cannot be dropped on the 1st or 8th ranks.
  • Pieces dropped retain their movements and values. A promoted pawn reverts back to a pawn when captured.

4. Win Conditions

The game is played in real-time. The match ends immediately when a player is checkmated or runs out of time on their clock. Since it is a team game, if either board wins or loses, the entire team wins or loses.
