The Curriculum

Master the game, one phase at a time.

01

The Mechanics

  • Move Accuracy

    Mastering piece movement, captures, and special rules (Castling, En Passant).

  • The Goal

    Identifying Checkmate vs. Stalemate to avoid "winning" games ending in a draw.

  • Board Sight

    Learning to see the whole board and recognize when a piece is "hanging" (undefended).

02

The Fundamentals

  • Opening Principles

    Controlling the center, developing pieces, and King safety.

  • The Big 3 Tactics

    Identifying and executing Forks, Pins, and Skewers.

  • Calculation

    Learning to think 2 moves ahead before touching a piece.
